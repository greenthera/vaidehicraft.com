Canearth15 Pop-Up Store

Step into timeless elegance and craftsmanship with Canearth15 by Vaidehi Craft. This exclusive pop-up store unveils a curated collection of exquisite cane furniture that celebrates traditional craftsmanship blended with modern aesthetics.

Designed with a boho-chic vibe, the store offers an immersive experience, showcasing furniture in a setting that highlights its beauty and functionality. From intricate cane weaves to innovative designs, every piece is a testament to Vaidehi Craft's dedication to quality, sustainability and elegance.

Cane is the poetry of luxury, a material that bends gracefully into forms bamboo could only dream of. At Vaidehi Craft, we take this exceptional natural wonder and sculpt it into art—functional, elegant, and timeless.

This isn’t just furniture; it’s an ode to artistry, an experience of exclusivity. Step beyond the ordinary and into the extraordinary.

Siddharrtha Shahh

Cane Maven

Siddharrtha Shahh, founder of Vaidehi Craft, has redefined cane furniture by blending traditional craftsmanship with modern aesthetics since 1998. With a passion for artistry and sustainability, he champions eco-conscious practices, using sustainable materials and supporting local artisans.

Under his leadership, the brand has grown from a local enterprise to a global symbol of excellence, known for its innovative and elegant designs. Siddharrtha's commitment to preserving heritage, uplifting artisan communities, and promoting social responsibility has made him a respected figure in art and craftsmanship.

His journey exemplifies resilience, determination, and a visionary approach, transforming Vaidehi Craft into a benchmark for quality, innovation, and sustainability.
