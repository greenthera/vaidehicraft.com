Cane furniture for a warm, inviting home

Bring natural textures, thoughtful furniture arrangements, and personal details together for a home that feels like yours.

By Vaidehi Craft

In this story

  1. Begin with the way you live
  2. Let woven detail breathe
  3. Make room for movement
  4. Finish with familiar things
← All stories

01

Begin with the way you live

Before choosing furniture, think about what you want the room to do. Is it a place for conversation, a quiet retreat, or somewhere the whole family gathers? Let those everyday moments guide the arrangement.

Start with one piece you love. A woven chair can give a reading corner its character, while a sofa can anchor a larger seating area. There is no need to furnish every corner at once.

02

Let woven detail breathe

Pair cane furniture with simpler fabrics so the weave remains a focal point. Cream, sand, and muted green offer a gentle starting palette. A patterned cushion or a favourite throw can bring a little contrast.

Repeat a colour in two or three places rather than adding many competing accents. A cushion, an artwork, and a small decorative object can connect different parts of the room.

03

Make room for movement

Measure the room and mark the proposed furniture positions before deciding. Consider doorways, windows, and the routes people use every day. A beautiful arrangement should also feel comfortable to move through.

Give side tables a useful position beside seating. Keep frequently used objects within reach, and leave room to pull a chair out without interrupting the rest of the space.

04

Finish with familiar things

Books, artwork, and objects collected over time make a room feel personal. Mix new furniture with pieces you already enjoy instead of trying to replace everything.

Ask about the care and placement requirements of the exact material, finish, and upholstery you choose. This is especially useful when furnishing a sunny window area or a balcony.
