Make space for a slower moment

Plan a swing corner with careful placement, comfortable surroundings, and the right installation advice.

By Vaidehi Craft

In this story

  1. Start with the location
  2. Discuss support and installation
  3. Choose for the actual environment
  4. Keep the surroundings simple
← All stories

01

Start with the location

Decide where the swing will be used before choosing a design. Think about walkways, nearby windows, and the furniture around it. A quieter corner can make a pleasant place to pause.

Do not judge the space only from the footprint of the seat. Ask about the clearance required for the specific swing and how it is intended to move.

02

Discuss support and installation

A suspended design and a freestanding design have different requirements. Confirm the support, fittings, installation process, and product limits with the supplier before making a decision.

For a suspended swing, have the proposed structural support assessed by a qualified professional. Do not assume a decorative ceiling or an existing hook is suitable.

03

Choose for the actual environment

A covered balcony can still be exposed to rain, humidity, and sunlight. Ask whether the exact frame, finish, and cushions are suitable for that position.

If the proposed location is unsuitable, consider an indoor setting instead. Placement should follow the guidance for the particular piece you choose.

04

Keep the surroundings simple

A nearby table and a cushion may be all the corner needs. Keep objects and loose cables away from the movement area, and leave access clear.

Check the finished arrangement with the installation team. Follow the supplied use and care guidance so the corner remains enjoyable in everyday life.
